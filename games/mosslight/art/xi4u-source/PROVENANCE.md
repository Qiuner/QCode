The TypeScript files are copied unchanged for reproducible offline conversion.
LICENSE.txt contains the original license. agent-isles's adaptations live in
../convert_streamside.mjs and ../../scripts/streamside.gd, not in these files.
agent-isles's additional architecture lives in ../streamside_garden.mjs: a layered
hipped roof replaces the original roof, a stone bridge replaces the old footbridge,
and a moon gate, tea pavilion, garden paths, lanterns and flower beds extend the layout.
The original summer sleeper is excluded from the exported geometry.

The converter builds a summer snapshot, bakes instances and colors, batches geometry,
and adds a tile foundation, shoreline extensions, connecting bridge, and veranda ramp.
Godot supplies lighting, movement, collision, water currents, fish paths and wind chime motion.
Original browser UI, weather, seasonal progression, custom water shaders, pixel effects,
and browser audio are not included in this adaptation.
