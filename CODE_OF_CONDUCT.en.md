# Community Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · **English**

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a harassment-free environment for everyone who communicates or contributes to agent-isles, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and respectful of different experiences and perspectives.
- Discuss problems and evidence without attacking people.
- Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for your impact.
- Protect others' privacy; do not disclose credentials, private communications, or identifying information.
- Exercise particular care in discussions involving minors, learners, or generated content.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized language or imagery, sustained unwelcome contact, disclosure of private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code applies to the repository, Issues, pull requests, Discussions, release channels, and other public or private spaces where someone represents the agent-isles community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public Issue. Contact the maintainer through the private reporting entry on the repository's **Security** page and identify the report as a community conduct incident. The maintainer must enable GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting before the repository is made public.

The maintainer will respect reporters' privacy and may issue a warning, edit or remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove someone based on the context. Malicious or knowingly false reports may also result in action.
